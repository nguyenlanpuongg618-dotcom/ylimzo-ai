# Ylimzo AI

**An educational workspace for AI-assisted crypto research, market notes, research checklists, backtesting basics, and risk awareness.**

---

## Welcome to Ylimzo AI

Ylimzo AI is an educational research project focused on helping people organize crypto market information in a clearer and more careful way.

Crypto research can become messy very quickly. Information may come from project websites, whitepapers, charts, market data, news articles, social media posts, community discussions, videos, and personal notes.

After collecting information from many places, it can become difficult to separate useful research from hype, assumptions, or incomplete information.

Ylimzo AI is built around one simple idea:

> AI should help organize research, not replace human judgment.

This project is not a trading signal service, not a financial adviser, not a price prediction tool, and not a guaranteed profit system.

The focus is education, documentation, research structure, and risk awareness.

---

## What Ylimzo AI Helps With

Ylimzo AI is designed as a learning-focused research workflow.

It can help organize ideas around:

- crypto research notes
- project review checklists
- market research summaries
- risk awareness templates
- beginner-friendly crypto education
- trading bot concepts
- backtesting basics
- safer AI prompt examples
- responsible AI use

The goal is not to tell users what to buy or sell.

The goal is to help users ask better questions and document their thinking more clearly.

---

## Why Research Structure Matters

Many people start crypto research with questions like:

- Should I buy this coin?
- Will Bitcoin go up tomorrow?
- Which token will increase next?

These questions focus on prediction.

A more careful research process asks:

- What information have I checked?
- What sources am I relying on?
- What assumptions am I making?
- What risks am I ignoring?
- What information is still missing?
- What would make me change my opinion?
- Am I following evidence or hype?

Ylimzo AI is built around this second approach.

---

## Example Research Workflow

A simple Ylimzo AI workflow could look like this:

1. Choose a crypto project, market topic, or research question.
2. Collect notes from different sources.
3. Separate facts, opinions, assumptions, and open questions.
4. List positive arguments.
5. List negative arguments.
6. Identify visible risks.
7. Mark missing or unverified information.
8. Write a temporary conclusion.
9. Review the conclusion later when new information is available.

This workflow does not guarantee a correct conclusion. It only makes the research process easier to review.

## Responsible AI Use

AI can help organize research, but AI can also make mistakes.

AI-generated information may be incomplete, outdated, inaccurate, biased, or overly confident.

For that reason, AI output should be treated as a starting point for review, not as a final answer.

Ylimzo AI encourages users to verify information independently and avoid using AI output as financial advice.
